Everything a student tracks, in one place.

Six focused tools that share one workspace, one look, and one place to look.

  • Tasks & Assignments

    Priorities, deadlines and status. Overdue, due today and upcoming are worked out from the date — never a stale label.

  • Calendar & Timetable

    Your classes on a weekly grid and your deadlines on a month view. Room and instructor are optional — add them only if you want them.

  • Quick Notes

    Formulas, a lecturer’s aside, a checklist. Filed by subject and searchable as you type.

  • CGPA Calculator

    Credit-weighted across every subject, on a 10-point scale with real failure states — E1, E2 and E3, each shown separately.

  • Attendance Calculator

    Plus and minus as classes happen, and a straight answer: how many you can still miss, or how many in a row you must attend.

  • Achievements

    Earned from what you actually did — tasks finished before their deadline, notes written, a streak kept.

Plus a unified dashboard, light & dark themes, and your data kept private on your own device.

Three steps between opening Synora and having your week in one place.

Questions, answered

Is Synora free?

Yes — completely. There's no paid tier, no trial period, and nothing locked behind an upgrade.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Synora is built around your account — it's what separates your tasks, notes and grades from anyone else using the same browser, and it's how the app knows what to load when you come back. Creating one takes a username and a password, and nothing else: no email, no phone number, no verification step.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage, on the device you're using — your account included. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The trade-off is that your workspace doesn't follow you between browsers or devices, and clearing your browser data will clear it.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Synora is responsive and runs in any modern mobile browser. Since data is stored per-device, you'll sign up again on your phone and it keeps its own copy.
